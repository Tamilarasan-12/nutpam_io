Travel Notes & Fare Estimate

  • Ask for an Anna University / BIT Campus ticket when boarding.

How to reach by bus

Route 1 · From Trichy

Board the bus at Trichy Central Bus Stand or Chathiram Bus Stand on the Pudukkottai / Keeranur route. Get down directly at the Anna University / BIT Campus stop.

TRICHY BUS → BIT CAMPUS STOP → B/C BLOCK

Route 2 · From Keeranur

Board the bus at Keeranur Bus Stop taking the Trichy direction / BIT Campus route. Get down directly at the Anna University / BIT Campus stop.

KEERANUR BUS → BIT CAMPUS STOP → B/C BLOCK
